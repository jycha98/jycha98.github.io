Hi, I'm a Ph.D. student in Machine Learning at Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Faramarz Fekri.

I earned my M.S. in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University, advised by Sungzoon Cho, and my B.S. in Electrical Engineering at KAIST with a minor in Business & Technology Management.

I'm currently interested in memory mechanisms for LLM agents, and how they can learn continually from their own experience at inference time.

Previously, I worked on efficient LLM inference algorithms, spanning speculative decoding, long-context generation, and KV-cache management.

My research is grounded in the belief that inference-time techniques are key to unlocking both the flexibility and performance of LLMs.
